Privacy Policy

Updated 28 October 2026. PickleCut is provided by SWINK AI INC.

Account information

When you sign in with Apple, Google or email, we use your account identifier, email address and the name or profile image you choose to share to authenticate you and provide your profile. Google sign-in requests only basic identity, email and profile access. We do not request access to your Gmail messages, contacts or Drive files.

Supabase provides account authentication and stores your synced profile, including any profile photo you upload. Login credentials and sessions are processed to keep your account signed in. Apple and Google process their respective sign-in flows under their own privacy policies.

Videos and device permissions

Your imported videos, edits and analysis results stay on your iPhone. Video analysis runs locally. Account sync does not upload your game videos. Camera, microphone and Photos permissions are used for recording, importing, saving and sharing. Sharing passes the export you select to the destination app you choose.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your profile, log out or delete your account from Edit profile. Deleting your account removes the account and its synced profile from our active account service. Local videos and exports are separate: remove them from the app or Photos where they are saved. You may also contact us for account or data questions.

Service providers and security

We use Supabase for authentication and profile storage and Vercel to host this website. These services may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the service. We do not sell your personal information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Contact and updates

Contact hello@goswie.com. We will update this page when our practices change.
