What can I do with PickleCut?+

Turn pickleball footage into highlight videos: review suggested rallies, trim clips, add music, customize ball tracers and edit a score overlay.

Is PickleCut available yet?+

PickleCut for iPhone is currently in development and testing. Contact us below for availability updates. There is no public App Store download yet.

How should I record my game?+

Use a fixed camera with the full court visible, ideally behind the baseline. Good light, a stable view and a visible ball help the analysis. You can import footage or use the app’s Live recording flow.

Does it keep score automatically?+

Scores are entered manually. You can edit team names, points, games and serving information in Studio. Automatic rally and tracer results should also be reviewed before exporting.

Where do my videos go?+

Video analysis and editing run locally on your iPhone. Account sign-in syncs your profile, not your game videos. You choose which exports to save or share.

Can I share my highlights?+

Yes. Save the exported video to your iPhone or use the system share sheet to send it to a compatible app. You can also upload the saved video to your favorite social platform.
